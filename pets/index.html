Weird Pets I'd Like To Get

Posted on October 1, By Kyleigh T
All Posts

I think the average house pets like cats, dogs etc are great and I love them but I think there are many other pets that deserve the same love as the most common ones. My blog post will go into my top favorites.

#1 Jumping Spiders

I am terrified of most spiders but I love jumping spiders. Personally I think they are so cute and they would most definitely be the only spider I would consider getting. The enclosure you would need is also relatively small so you can easily find or make space for it.

Jumping spiders are also considered extremely low maintenance and the most you have to do for it daily is mist the sides of its enclosure. The average jumping spider costs $30-60 with no enclosure or starter items and with an enclosure and starter items it should only cost $60-100.

This pet would be great as a starter pet for many and I think they are so worth buying.This pet would be great as a starter pet for many and I think they are so worth buying.

#2 Ball Pythons

Ball Pythons are a calmer snake compared to some others but they can get a bit pricey to buy outright. I personally think the price is worth it if you like snakes and aren't scared of them.

Ball pythons require moderate work and do ask for a bit more than what a jumping spider would need but the daily maintenance is still low. They are a great pet if you have allergies. It is more likely to be scratched by your cat than it is for the snake to bite you if you are worried about getting bit. They also have a long lifespan of like 20-30 years!

#3 Chinchila's

Chinchillas are a type of rodent with the softest fur in the world. They have around 50-80 hairs per follicle unlike us in which we only have one per follicle. I also love the fact they have a long life span especially for a rodent.

They also produce low dander which is great if you have pet allergies. One thing to note is they are prone to overheat and it could be fatal if they aren't in a properly air conditioned room under 75 degrees. They are pretty quiet too and independent for the most part as well..

Those are only three pets but the list below is all the animals i'd consider getting

  1. Jumping Spiders
  2. Ball Pythons
  3. Chinchillas
  4. Corn snakes
  5. African Fat Tailed Gecko
  6. Bush baby
  7. Capybara
  8. Ring Tailed Cat
  9. Jerboa
  10. Serval
